Grounded in guidance

Built around strength—not age stereotypes.

Public-health guidance recommends muscle-strengthening activity for adults, including older adults. Stronger Years turns that broad recommendation into a coached introduction to strength training.

“Muscle-strengthening activities should be done at moderate or greater intensity and involve all major muscle groups on 2 or more days a week.”

World Health Organization, Guidelines on Physical Activity and Sedentary Behaviour (2020). Read the guidance ↗

This is general public-health guidance, not an individual prescription. Exercise should be adapted to each participant’s needs and health circumstances.

Good questions

Before you begin.

Do I need experience with weights?

No. The programme is intended as a beginner-friendly introduction. Tell the team about your experience so the coach can help identify an appropriate starting point.

What if I have an injury or health concern?

Contact the gym before booking and discuss your circumstances with an appropriately qualified professional. The team should explain what adaptations are possible and whether you need clearance from your healthcare professional before participating.

What is included in the DEXA scan?

Scan availability, provider, timing, interpretation and any associated costs must be confirmed by your local gym. A DEXA scan measures specific body-composition or bone-related outcomes; it does not directly measure all aspects of strength, technique or everyday function.

How much does the programme cost?

The final price has not yet been confirmed. Ask your local gym for the full eight-week price and an itemised explanation of sessions, assessments and any additional charges before you commit.

What happens after the eight weeks?

You can review what you have learned with the coach and discuss suitable next steps, including continuing with small-group training or another appropriate programme. Ongoing membership is not assumed to be included.
